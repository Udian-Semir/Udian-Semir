关于我 About

从高中阶段开始参与机器人与无人机项目，持续接触定位、导航、视觉、自瞄、强化学习和机械臂操作。 My robotics work began in high school and now spans localization, navigation, vision, auto-aiming, reinforcement learning, and manipulation.

目前在 RoboMaster 团队参与算法研发，并在 Synthoid 参与机械臂遥操作、数据生成与具身智能真机部署。 I currently work on algorithms in RoboMaster and have contributed to teleoperation, data generation, and embodied-AI deployment at Synthoid.
